Crew shifts

How to play

  1. Your crew has a few work shifts each day. Pick a build from the bottom bar, then press Do it.
  2. Watch the top bar. If battery, oxygen, food or morale hit zero, or radiation passes the budget, the mission ends.
  3. The strip under it shows sunny and dark days. Plan ahead, then press End day.

Moonbase Commander

Four astronauts. Thirty days on the Moon. Every fix costs something somewhere else. Can you keep them alive?

Solar flares come from NASA's DONKI database when your browser can reach it. Other numbers are simplified for play. Student project, not endorsed by NASA. Code generated with AI assistance (Claude, by Anthropic). Fonts: Fredoka and Nunito (Google Fonts).
